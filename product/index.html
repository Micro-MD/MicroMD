The MicroMD™ Time Reverse Cream works on all areas showing signs of aging: face, neck, eyes, lips, forehead, and jawline. Perfect for targeting fine lines, uneven tone, and loss of firmness. You can also apply it to areas with rough texture or dryness.

No added fragrance. The MicroMD formula is nearly odorless, with only a very light, natural scent from centella and botanical oils that fades quickly upon application.

The MicroMD™ Time Reverse Cream targets fine lines, wrinkles, dark spots, loss of firmness, and dullness all at once. Therefore, complicated routines are not needed! Many users find this is the only cream they need for visible, youthful-looking results and radiant, glowing skin. Just cleanse, apply, and enjoy the results!
