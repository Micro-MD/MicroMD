MicroMD product sections: Shopify code

One tap copies everything you need.

  1. In Shopify, open Online Store → Themes → Customize and go to your product page.
  2. Below your Kaching Bundles block, click Add section (or Add block) → Custom Liquid.
  3. Delete anything already in the box, paste, and click Save.
  4. Check the live product page (the editor preview may not run the slider).
